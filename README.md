## Who I Am

I'm **Saif Ullah** — a Data Scientist and AI/ML Engineer from Pakistan who also builds full-stack applications. I work across the entire pipeline: from cleaning raw data and training transformer models to wiring up RAG pipelines and shipping production web apps.

Most of my recent work sits at the intersection of **LLMs and real software** — agents that automate workflows, NLP systems that summarize and extract meaning, and ML models that get deployed rather than just trained. I care about systems that actually run, not just notebooks that look good.

---

## What I Specialize In

- **Data Science & ML** — end-to-end pipelines, EDA, feature engineering, model selection, evaluation, and production deployment
- **AI/ML Engineering** — PyTorch, HuggingFace Transformers, LangChain, Agno, RAG pipelines, fine-tuning, RL agents
- **Full-Stack Development** — MERN stack, Laravel, FastAPI, Flask, REST APIs, JWT auth, role-based systems
- **Applied AI** — Computer vision (OpenCV), NLP, vector databases, LLM-powered applications
